Common questions

What stays free?

Most of the app: the map, Today’s five, search, notes and follow-ups, the federal map, pursuits, pipeline, recompetes, teaming partners and SAM.gov notices. Free isn’t a trial. It doesn’t run out.

I bought lifetime Pro. Do I pay for Team?

Yes, for the seat. Team is its own subscription, so lifetime Pro stays lifetime and never changes. A seat pays for the shared team features.

Can my company pay?

Yes. Team on the web bills through Stripe with invoices, which suits a company card or accounts payable. Pro bought through Apple or Google comes with a receipt you can expense.

What about refunds?

Refunds for Pro go through Apple or Google. For Team on the web, see the refund terms.

Does any plan send my network to Northfix?

No. Every plan keeps your network on your devices and in your own iCloud. Team shares are encrypted on your device before they leave it, so the team server can’t read them.
