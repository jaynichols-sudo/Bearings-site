See your coverage across the government

Northfix matches each person’s employer and title to 160 departments, agencies and military commands. Each one shows as covered, thin or not connected. Covered means you know someone senior and someone you’re close to. Thin means one or the other.

  • Gaps first. Filter to the agencies where you know no one, by focus area: defense, cyber, energy, water, infrastructure, transportation or intelligence.
  • Old names still count. Agencies get renamed and merged. A contact listed at the old office still matches the new one.
  • Watch an agency. Leadership changes and news for the agencies you follow come to you.

Map who decides each bid

A pursuit in Northfix has a seat for each person who shapes the award. Northfix suggests who in your network fits each seat, and shows your warmest path to the empty ones.

Government seats

Executive sponsor, program manager, contracting officer, mission owner, security lead and technical lead.

Industry seats

The incumbent and your teaming partners, with who you know at each company.

Cold seats

A warning when you haven’t talked to someone in a seat for 60 days and the due date is close.

Pipeline

Open value by stage, weighted by the chance of winning each pursuit, plus what’s due this quarter and your win rate.

Call plans

Before a call: what the person owns, what changed, what you last talked about, and what to ask. Written on your device.

One-page pursuit PDF

Seats, who you know in each and the warmest ways in, on one page for a gate review.

Recompetes and teaming partners

Northfix reads public contract awards from USASpending.gov, straight from your device. No key needed.

  • Recompete radar. Contracts ending in the next 24 months at the agencies you work, with the incumbent, the value, the end date, and who you know at both the agency and the incumbent.
  • Teaming partner finder. The companies that win work at an agency, biggest first, with who you and your team know at each.
  • Only the agency name is sent. USASpending.gov never learns who is in your network.

SAM.gov notices, matched to who you know

Add your own free SAM.gov key and Northfix watches for sources sought, presolicitations and solicitations at the agencies where you already know people. Each notice shows who you know at the buying office and how well. When a notice posts where you know three or more people, your phone tells you. Your key stays on your device. How to get a key.
