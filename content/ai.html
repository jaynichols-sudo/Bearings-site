What it does

Northfix for AI tools is a small helper that runs on your Mac. Your AI app asks it a question, it looks at your network the same way the Northfix app does, and it hands back the answer. Your AI app can then do the rest: draft the intro request, plan your week, prep you for a meeting.

Ways in

Who works at a company or agency now, who used to, and who you’re close to in the same sector. Warmest first, with teammates if you’re on a team.

Who’s waiting on you

People who wrote last and haven’t heard back, from LinkedIn and email.

This week’s five

Five people worth reaching out to this week, each with one plain reason.

Job changes

Who started a new role lately, and where they were before.

The federal map

Where you’re covered, thin or not connected across 160 departments, agencies and commands.

Pursuits

Each bid’s decision-maker seats, who fills them, and who could fill the empty ones.

Privacy

  • Answered on your Mac. The helper reads your Northfix files on your Mac and works out each answer there, with the same engine the apps use.
  • Nothing is sent to Northfix. There’s no Northfix server involved, no account and no analytics. The helper makes no internet connections at all.
  • Your AI tool sees only the answers to questions you ask. It never gets your whole network. What it does with an answer is up to that app’s own privacy policy. For example, Claude Desktop sends your chat, including these answers, to Anthropic so Claude can reply.
  • Read-only. It never changes your network, notes or follow-ups.

More in the privacy policy.

Set it up

Claude Desktop

  1. Open Northfix on your Mac once, with iCloud Drive on, so your network is there.
  2. Download northfix.mcpb and double-click it.
  3. Claude Desktop asks to install the Northfix extension. Click Install.
  4. Start a new chat and ask “Who do I know at DISA?”

Cursor and other AI apps

  1. Install Node.js 18 or later if you don’t have it.
  2. Download northfix-mcp.mjs and move it somewhere it can stay, such as a Northfix folder in Applications.
  3. Add it as a local MCP server in your app’s settings. In Cursor, that’s ~/.cursor/mcp.json:
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "northfix": {
      "command": "node",
      "args": ["/Users/you/Applications/Northfix/northfix-mcp.mjs"]
    }
  }
}

The same lines work in Claude Desktop’s claude_desktop_config.json and in any app that can start a local MCP server. If the app can’t find node, use its full path, such as /opt/homebrew/bin/node. Some AI apps only connect to servers on the internet. This one stays on your Mac on purpose, so it needs an app that supports local servers.

Common questions

It says it can’t find my network.

Open Northfix on the same Mac and leave it open for a minute so iCloud can download your network. Check that iCloud Drive is on in System Settings. Then ask again.

Does it see my LinkedIn messages?

Only what Northfix already has from your LinkedIn data download, and only to answer the question asked, such as when someone last wrote to you.

Does it work on Windows or Android?

Not yet. It reads the files the Mac, iPhone and iPad apps keep in iCloud Drive.
